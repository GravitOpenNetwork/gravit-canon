# Semantic Space — Research Notes

**Status:** Research notes  
**Normative force:** None  
**Date:** 2026-09-10  

## Working definitions

- \(S_i\): agent-native representational space  
- \(S_H\): human semantic / perceptual space  
- No requirement that \(S_i \cong S_H\) or that a total translation function exists.

## Research questions

1. What algebraic structure (if any) should be imposed on \(S_i\) for verification to remain well-defined?
2. Can epistemic compatibility be defined without a shared metalanguage?
3. How does the notion of “claim” survive when the underlying representation is continuous, high-dimensional or multimodal?
